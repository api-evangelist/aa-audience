## What the company does

- **Credit-score estimation for mobile users.** AA Audience states it can estimate a mobile user's
  credit score from that user's online activity, using proprietary machine learning models.
- **Data supply.** Data is collected from partner mobile apps and ad networks.
- **Distribution.** Scored audiences are made available in mainstream programmatic infrastructure —
  the site names AdX, OATH, Rubicon, OpenX, LiveRamp and BlueKai.
- **Buyers.** Banks, credit card companies and financial services firms, buying audiences for
  credit-card, loan and financial-services campaigns.
- **Segments.** The advertisers page claims over 100 segments, keyed on attributes such as
  estimated credit score, number of credit cards, and mortgage status.

## Two sides of the marketplace

- **Publishers** (https://aaaudi.com/publishers.html) — mobile websites and apps drop a JavaScript
  header-bidding tag; AA Audience enriches the bid stream with user attributes so those users clear
  at higher eCPM. No tag URL, snippet or integration documentation is published.
- **Advertisers** (https://aaaudi.com/advertisers.html) — buyers state an audience goal, and
  AA Audience either narrows a segment inside the buyer's DMP or creates a DealID for the buyer's
  DSP. Stated rate floor: $1 CPM.

## Compliance and privacy posture

AA Audience publishes **no privacy policy, no terms of service, no opt-out page and no
security.txt** — `/privacy.html`, `/terms.html`, `/opt-out.html` and
`/.well-known/security.txt` all 403. The domain has no DNSSEC, no CAA, no SPF and no DMARC record,
and does not send HSTS. This is recorded as measured fact about a company whose stated product is
inferring consumer credit attributes from mobile behavioural data.
